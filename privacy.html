Privacy & Support

Private by default.

Your assessment answers, symptom and food logs, and profile are stored on your device. Limited purchase, network, diagnostic, and support information may be handled by the providers described below.

Last updated: September 6, 2026

The short version: the health-adjacent information you enter, such as symptoms, meals, assessment answers, and profile details, is stored locally on your device and is not sent to a SiboMate account or application server. Some supporting services handle limited information so purchases, optional AI, downloads, crash diagnostics, and support can work.

What information the app handles

Stored on your device only

  • Your assessment answers and results, including breath-test values and symptom checks.
  • Your symptom and food logs from the Tracker.
  • Your profile details and app preferences.
  • Any AI provider API key you enter, which is kept in the secure device keychain.

This information is encrypted at rest in the app’s local storage. API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. We do not operate an account system or application server that receives these records.

The research database

The app can search a database of publicly posted, community-reported experiences from health communities. The database is downloaded from a content-hosting provider and searched locally after download. The provider may receive standard network information, such as your IP address, device or browser information, and request time, when the file is downloaded. Local database searches do not send your search terms to us.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or create a SiboMate account.
  • We do not include third-party advertising or use your health logs for cross-app advertising.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not receive your symptom, meal, assessment, or profile records on a SiboMate server.

AI features and your health information

The AI feature is optional and turned off until you enable it. Before you can use it, the app shows a consent screen explaining what will be shared, and you must agree.

When you ask the AI a question, the app sends your question and relevant context, which may include assessment results, logs, and matching research excerpts, directly to the AI provider you choose. The request uses the API key you entered and does not pass through a SiboMate application server. The provider’s own privacy policy, retention practices, and terms apply. You can disconnect the AI and remove your key at any time.

AI answers are not medical advice. They can be incomplete or wrong. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making health decisions.

Purchases

SiboMate offers auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat.

RevenueCat processes purchase records, receipt information, an app-specific identifier, and standard technical information so purchases work across reinstalls. SiboMate does not intentionally send your name, email address, symptom logs, food logs, assessment answers, or profile details to RevenueCat. Read RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Crash diagnostics

If crash reporting is enabled in a released version, Sentry may process limited technical information, such as app version, device model, operating-system version, timestamps, and error details, to help us diagnose failures. SiboMate is configured not to attach symptom logs, food logs, assessment answers, AI prompts, or user-entered profile details to crash reports. Read Sentry’s privacy policy.

Support messages

If you email support, your email provider and Google may process your address, message, attachments, and related delivery information. We use that information to respond and troubleshoot. Please avoid including health information you do not want to send by email. Read Google’s privacy policy.

Children

SiboMate is not directed to children under 13. Users should meet the App Store age rating shown in their region. If you believe a child has sent information through support, contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Retention, deletion, and your choices

On-device app data remains until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Removing the app does not automatically delete records independently retained by Apple, RevenueCat, an AI provider, Sentry, a download host, or an email provider under their own policies. Support messages are kept only as reasonably needed to respond, maintain support history, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes. You can ask us to delete support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to records we must retain.

You can avoid AI-provider processing by leaving AI disconnected, remove your API key in the app, and avoid sending sensitive information in support email. If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a privacy question? Email sibomate@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

Purchases and subscriptions

To restore a purchase, open the purchase screen and choose “Restore Purchases.” To cancel a subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew. Prices, taxes, currencies, and trial eligibility can vary by region and are confirmed by Apple before purchase. Refund requests are handled under Apple’s policies.
