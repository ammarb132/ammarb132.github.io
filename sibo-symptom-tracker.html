Common questions

About the SIBO tracker.

What can I track in SiboMate?+

You can record symptoms and meals, then review those logs for patterns you may want to discuss with a qualified healthcare professional.

Does SiboMate diagnose SIBO?+

No. SiboMate is an educational organization and tracking tool. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional medical care.

Does SiboMate require an account?+

No. Symptom, meal, assessment, and profile data stay on your device. The optional AI feature sends information only when you choose to use it with your own provider key.

Can I use the research library offline?+

Yes. After the research database is downloaded, searches can run on your iPhone without an account.
